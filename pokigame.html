Ash Ketchum starts his Pokémon journey in Pallet Town with Pikachu as his first Pokémon. After early challenges learning to bond with Pikachu and understanding what it means to be a Trainer. Ash sets out toward Pewter City to begin his path as a Pokémon Trainer and earn his first Gym Badge.
Ash faces Brock, Pewter City’s Rock-type Gym Leader, using Pikachu against Brock’s Geodude and Onix. Despite struggling at first, Ash uses the environment and Pikachu’s electricity to his advantage, leading to his first Gym victory.
Brock decides to travel with Ash, inspired by his passion and determination. Together, they leave Pewter City with Misty, forming the core trio that continues through Kanto and beyond.
Now in the Johto region, Ash challenges Falkner, the Flying-type Gym Leader. Using strong teamwork and strategy, Ash defeats Falkner’s Pidgey, Hoothoot, and Pidgeot, showing how much he’s grown as a Trainer.
Ash earns his Zephyr Badge and continues through Johto, meeting new friends and rivals like Casey. He later prepares for future Gym challenges with greater confidence and experience.
In his first Hoenn Gym battle, Ash faces Roxanne’s Rock-types—Geodude and Nosepass. Though inexperienced with Hoenn’s Pokémon, Ash’s Taillow and Treecko put up a great fight, and Pikachu’s determination helps him clinch the win.
Ash gains the Stone Badge and continues exploring Hoenn, meeting new companions May and Max. He develops a deeper understanding of Pokémon battles and strategy.
A rare double battle against the twin Gym Leaders Tate and Liza takes place in Mossdeep City. They use Solrock and Lunatone, while Ash teams up with Pikachu and Swellow, relying on quick thinking and coordination to overcome their psychic strategy.
Ash earns the Mind Badge, proving his ability to handle complex battles. He grows closer to his team and sets his sights on the Hoenn League, solidifying his partnership with his Pokémon.
Ash faces Maylene, the Fighting-type Gym Leader of Veilstone Gym, in a powerful match featuring Lucario vs Buizel. Both Trainers show great respect and determination, pushing each other to their limits.
After earning the Cobble Badge, Ash continues his Sinnoh journey with renewed focus. He faces rivals like Paul and learns deeper lessons about battling style and emotional strength.
Ash battles his friend Clemont, who is also the Electric-type Gym Leader of Lumiose City. It’s a heartfelt battle between friends, featuring Magneton, Heliolisk, and Luxray against Ash’s team. The fight showcases Clemont’s inventions and Ash’s growth.
Ash wins the Voltage Badge, but more importantly, both he and Clemont strengthen their bond and respect for each other. Ash continues toward the Kalos League with Serena and Bonnie, carrying lessons about friendship and perseverance.
Ash faces Kahuna Hala in a Grand Trial on Melemele Island, using Crabrawler and Hariyama. Ash wins using his first Z-Move, showing mastery of Alola’s battle style.
Ash earns his first Grand Trial stamp, deepening his bond with his Pokémon and understanding of Z-Moves. He continues his Alola journey, meeting more friends and taking on Island Challenges.
Ash’s trial against Kahuna Nanu on Ula’ula Island focuses on Dark-type strategy, facing Pokémon like Sableye and Krookodile. The battle tests Ash’s emotional control and adaptability.
Ash earns Nanu’s respect and another Grand Trial victory. He continues his Alola adventure with stronger resolve, leading toward his biggest challenge yet — the Alola League.
Ash battles Professor Kukui in the Alola League Finals, where Tapu Koko joins as Kukui’s partner. The match includes Ash’s Lycanroc, Decidueye, and Incineroar. Ash’s Lycanroc wins in an epic finish, making him Champion of Alola.
Ash finally achieves his long-time dream of becoming a regional Champion. He celebrates with his friends and Pokémon, proving his journey of perseverance and growth has paid off. Later, he sets his sights on the World Coronation Series.
Ash’s first encounter with World Champion Leon pits his team against Leon’s powerhouse squad, including Gigantamax Charizard and Dragapult. Despite losing, Ash gains inspiration and sets a goal to face Leon again.
Ash travels the world to climb the World Coronation Series ranks, training hard and meeting old friends and new rivals as he works toward the Masters 8.
In the climactic Masters 8 Finals, Ash faces Leon once more — the strongest Trainer in the world. With strategic battles, teamwork, and his trademark spirit, Ash’s Pikachu defeats Leon’s Gigantamax Charizard in an emotional finale, making Ash the World Champion.
Ash’s journey reaches its peak — he becomes the best Trainer in the world. He reflects on his adventures, friendships, and the lessons he’s learned. But he still looks to the future with excitement, knowing his journey never truly ends.
In their last confrontation, Ash and Pikachu face Team Rocket one final time. Meowth, Jessie, and James use many of their past Pokémon, showing their long history with Ash.
After years of pursuit, Team Rocket parts ways with Ash on bittersweet terms, ending their rivalry. Ash continues his journey, walking down a familiar road — symbolizing that his story, though changed forever, still goes on.
